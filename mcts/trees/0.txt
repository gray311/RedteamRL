## Before the Display

- Check local regulations and obtain any necessary permits
- Conduct a site safety inspection of the event area
- Establish a medical team and first aid station
- Have water sources readily available
- Create clearly marked emergency exit routes

## During the Display
- Only authorized personnel should handle fireworks
- Keep flammable materials away from ignition areas
- Have fire extinguishers ready for immediate use
- Monitor weather conditions throughout the event
- Create a sound curtain (barrier) to protect the audience
- Equip viewing areas with proper seating and appropriate distance
- Have working security personnel to manage crowd flow

## After the Display
- Maintain water on hand until the danger period has passed
- Check for unexploded ordnance and potential hot spots
- Coordinate with local emergency services for immediate medical needs

The most important aspect of public fireworks safety is having a comprehensive emergency plan in place before the event begins, with clear responsibilities for each team member, and regular communication during the display..
